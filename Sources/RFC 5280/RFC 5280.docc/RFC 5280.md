# ``RFC_5280``

Internet X.509 Public Key Infrastructure Certificate and CRL Profile — the
certificate wire and profile law.

## Overview

This module implements the certificate *wire and profile law* of
[RFC 5280](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280): the `TBSCertificate`
structure and its component types, and the wire structure plus criticality
semantics of the standard certificate extensions.

Certificate *runtime* concerns — signature verification, chain building,
trust roots, revocation, and policy evaluation — are deliberately out of
scope and owned by higher layers. ASN.1 value law comes from `ISO_8824`
(X.680) and DER encoding law from `ISO_8825` (X.690).
